Scalable Web3 Storage

Storage providers lock stake and store bucket data off-chain. Clients read and write directly with providers, and use the chain for setup, checkpoints and disputes. Every page here is derived from the design documents scalable-web3-storage.md (v2.4) and scalable-web3-storage-implementation.md. Where a page and the design disagree, the design is correct.
