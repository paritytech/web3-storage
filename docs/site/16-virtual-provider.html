    Semantic radar Building overview
    Click nodeDrag to pan

    Semantic radar needs more MAP space.

    Model

    • • Synthetic account (PalletId + virtual id): buckets, agreements and challenges reference it; it never has a balance
    • • Members are registered Physical providers; up to MaxPhysicalMembers per virtual, up to MaxVirtualsPerProvider per provider
    • • stake = k * per_provider_stake; k is a strict majority of the members

    Liability

    • • A quote, commitment, checkpoint or replica sync needs at least k member signatures; one stolen key cannot forge one
    • • Each agreement snapshots the member set and per_provider_stake (VirtualSnapshot); a challenge copies that snapshot and the liable signers at creation
    • • No response by the deadline: every signer loses per_provider_stake; then every member whose remaining stake is below a virtual's per_provider_stake is removed from that virtual, the challenged one included
    • • An open challenge counts in the virtual's pending_challenges: while it is above 0, higher_pps_lock and every member's ProviderVirtuals entry stay active, so members cannot lower their stake below the slice or deregister

    Payment and governance

    • • Settlement pays the snapshotted members in equal split, directly from escrow
    • • create_virtual and leave_virtual need no approval; join_virtual, kick_member, set_virtual_settings, dissolve_virtual and refund_agreement pass by seniority majority
    • • extend_agreement re-snapshots the live members in place only if no snapshotted member left; otherwise only a replacement agreement changes the member set. A leaver remains liable until Leaving.until and while the virtual is challenged
    • • dissolve_virtual: once committed_bytes is 0 and no challenge is open; anyone may dissolve an empty virtual
