    No downtime

    • • Both primaries are in bucket.primary_providers until the old agreement ends
    • • The new primary signs a checkpoint while the old one is still bound
    • • A free slot under MaxPrimaryProviders is required

    The client moves the data

    • • Primaries do not sync with each other
    • • A client that keeps a local copy only uploads
    • • Otherwise the change costs a full download and a full upload

    Ending the old agreement

    • • After expires_at: owner settles with end_agreement, or the provider calls claim_expired_agreement after SettlementTimeout
    • • Before expiry: the admin ends it with end_agreement; the full remaining payment is paid or burned
    • • Blocked while a challenge against the old primary is unresolved

    Not current behaviour

    • • Provider-to-provider fetch is a Future Direction in the design
    • • Today the new primary gets the data only from the client
