    Semantic radar needs more MAP space.

    Three challenge modes

    • • challenge_checkpoint: a provider in the current snapshot; for cold buckets
    • • challenge_offchain: a provider-signed Commitment; for hot buckets
    • • challenge_replica: the root the replica confirmed in last_sync
    • • Use a challenge after off-chain requests fail

    Who may challenge

    • • Any signed account except the challenged provider (SelfChallenge)
    • • Private bucket, primary provider: member or primary-agreement owner only (NotAuthorizedForPrivateBucket)
    • • The gate reads the provider's role from its current agreement; replicas are open to anyone
    • • Tier: authorized (member or any agreement owner) or public, stored in Challenge.authorized at creation

    Authorized cost split, illustrative (challenger / provider)

    • • Block 1: 90% / 10%
    • • Blocks 2-5: 80% / 20%
    • • Blocks 6-24: 70% / 30%
    • • Blocks 25-95: 60% / 40%
    • • Blocks 96+: 50% / 50%
    • • Fixed rule: challenger pays at least 50%. Public challenger: 100% / 0%

    Responses and rejections

    • • Proof: chunk in leaf, leaf in committed MMR; else InvalidProof
    • • Deleted: admin-signed commitment with new_start_seq above the challenged leaf; else InvalidDeletionClaim
    • • Superseded: a newer canonical snapshot covers the leaf; else InvalidSupersededClaim
    • • A rejected response costs the provider its tx fee; the challenge stays open until the deadline
    • • No response by the deadline: anyone calls resolve_expired_challenge (free on success); only a timeout slashes

    After a slash

    • • The provider loses its full stake, not a per-bucket share
    • • The challenger gets the deposit and tx fees back, with no reward
    • • Anyone can call remove_slashed; the owner gets the remaining agreement payment back
