    Three hold reasons

    • • ProviderStake: provider collateral
    • • AgreementPayment: fee and sync_balance, on the owner; moves on transfer_agreement_ownership
    • • ChallengeDeposit: challenger deposit

    Stake and challenges

    • • Failed challenge: full stake slashed to the Treasury
    • • Challenger made whole from the slash, no reward
    • • Valid response: stake untouched
    • • complete_deregister releases the stake

    Who pays the response cost

    • • Provider pays the fee from its own account
    • • Public challenger: deposit reimburses 100%
    • • Authorized: split by response time, challenger pays at least 50%
