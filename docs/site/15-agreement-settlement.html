    Semantic radar needs more MAP space.

    While active

    • • top_up_agreement (owner only) raises max_bytes for the remaining duration
    • • extend_agreement: anyone if the current price is at or below the locked price, else owner only; fails once expired
    • • set_extensions_blocked lets the provider refuse extensions for one bucket
    • • transfer_agreement_ownership moves the escrow hold to the new owner; bucket membership stays

    Pay or burn

    • • EndAction::Burn { burn_percent } burns that share to Treasury and pays the rest
    • • A burn also charges the owner a governance-set premium; without the funds the burn fails
    • • After SettlementTimeout the provider calls claim_expired_agreement and the owner loses the burn option

    Early exits and guards

    • • Before expiry only a bucket admin can end an agreement, and only a primary; the full remaining payment is paid or burned
    • • Replica agreements cannot be ended early
    • • AgreementHasPendingChallenge blocks end_agreement and claim_expired_agreement
    • • Anyone can call remove_slashed; the remaining payment returns to the owner
